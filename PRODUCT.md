# How to Adult

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
ios

## Users
People looking for practical help with everyday life skills. The user described a pocket guide spanning laundry, cooking, taxes, car maintenance, savings, clothing, home decoration, exercise, friendship and reading. The product never frames its readers as having been failed by their parents.

## Product Purpose
Help someone find an answer, follow clear steps and keep useful guides for later. Publish more articles over time without requiring an app update.

## Operating Context
Quick questions during real tasks, followed by longer reading when useful. A bundled library remains available without an internet connection. Dynamic publication requires a verified content backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The existing Objective-C prototype displays placeholder prime numbers. Its empty Core Data model and legacy files are preserved. Modern native iPhone/iPad and Mac targets replace the prototype implementation. No visionOS builds or Simulator work are permitted under the user's explicit restriction. The requested future daily-article loop is not scheduled by this release.

## Brand Commitments
The name is How to Adult. Use minimal interface text, icons, color and hierarchy to make actions understandable. Avoid clipped text and condescending language. The user's native-editor preference informs this session's direct implementation with written decisions; it is not recorded as a new standing workflow default.

## Product Principles
- Find a useful answer quickly.
- Show actionable steps rather than motivational filler.
- Identify local rules and sources where they matter.
- Preserve saved progress during publication updates and offline use.
- Keep publication separate from personal guide progress.

## Open Decisions
US-first financial content and answer-first opening layout are provisional implementation assumptions, presented through optional questions on 2026-10-03. Everyday content remains generally useful internationally. No daily generation service or schedule has been requested yet.
